Formulas

Hemocytometer

cells/mL = (cells ÷ squares) × DF × 1000 ÷ V_square(µL) Neubauer: V_square = 0.1 µL → ×10⁴ DF = (sample + stain) ÷ sample × pre-dilution viability % = live ÷ (live + dead) × 100 total cells = cells/mL × suspension mL

Seeding

cells/vessel = density(cells/cm²) × area suspension/vessel = cells/vessel ÷ stock conc medium/vessel = final volume − suspension master mix = per-vessel × n × (1 + overage)

Fixed medium volume

keep final volume V: V_cells = C₂·V ÷ C₁ add on top of V: V_cells = C₂·V ÷ (C₁ − C₂) final = (V + V_cells), conc = N ÷ final

Dilution

C₁V₁ = C₂V₂ serial: transfer = V_tube ÷ fold, diluent = V_tube − transfer conc_k = C₀ ÷ fold^k

Growth

N(t) = N₀ × 2^((t − lag) ÷ Td) Td = Δt × ln2 ÷ ln(N₁ ÷ N₀) µ = ln(N₁ ÷ N₀) ÷ Δt PD = log₂(N₁ ÷ N₀) = 3.32 × log₁₀(N₁ ÷ N₀) time to target = Td × log₂(target ÷ N₀) + lag

Replicates

SD = √( Σ(x − mean)² ÷ (n − 1) ) CV % = SD ÷ mean × 100 Poisson error of a count ≈ √N (100 cells → ±10 %)
